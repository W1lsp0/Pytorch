Paper Submission
All papers must be submitted electronically via EDAS at https://edas.info/N35627. Submissions must not substantially overlap with previously published
papers or papers concurrently submitted to other journals or conferences. Full regular papers are allowed up to 8 complimentary pages, with up to 2
additional pages available for purchase (maximum 10 pages). Regular papers or special session papers are allowed up to 6 complimentary pages, with up
to 2 additional pages available for purchase (maximum 8 pages). All papers must be formatted in the IEEE Computer Society Proceedings Format
(templates available at https://template-selector.ieee.org/). All submitted papers will undergo a single-blind peer review process.
Paper Publication
Papers accepted by ISPA 2026 will be published by the IEEE Computer Society Press. All accepted papers will be submitted to IEEE Xplore and EI.
Distinguished papers presented at the conference, after further revision, will be invited for submission to a special issue.
