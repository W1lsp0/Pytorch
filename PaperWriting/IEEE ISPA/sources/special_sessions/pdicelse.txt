PDICEL-SE 2026
PDICEL-SE 2026: IEEE ISPA 2026 Special Session on Parallel and Distributed Intelligence, Cloud-Edge Computing, and Large Models for Smart Education
The rapid advance of generative large models is reshaping smart education and creating new requirements for low-latency response, massive data-parallel processing, and reliable distributed service support. However, traditional centralized educational platforms often struggle to support real-time classroom interaction, offline teaching services, and the privacy-preserving processing of student learning data.
This special session focuses on the convergence of parallel and distributed intelligence, cloud-edge collaborative computing, and large models for education. It aims to explore cutting-edge distributed AI technologies for smart education, bridge foundational theories of parallel and distributed computing with practical educational applications, and foster efficient, trustworthy, and scalable intelligent education systems.
This special session is now soliciting original research contributions from researchers and practitioners worldwide. We look forward to your submissions!
We invite submissions from academia, government and industry that present novel research on the topics as following areas:
Frontier Parallel and Distributed Intelligent Algorithms for Educational Scenarios
Cloud-Edge Collaborative System Architectures for Smart Education
Distributed Collaborative Inference of Large Language Models in Teaching Scenarios
Dynamic Task Offloading and Resource Scheduling for Educational Cloud-Edge Clusters
Lightweight Optimization and Edge Deployment of Domain-Specific Educational Large Models
Parallel Mining and Intelligent Analysis of Massive Student Learning Behavior Data
Distributed Adaptive Learning Systems Driven by Cloud-Edge Big Data
Low-Latency Interactive Intelligent Classrooms Based on Parallel Edge Computing
Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Distributed Educational Computing
Blockchain-Enabled Trusted Distributed Data Management for Smart Campuses
Energy-Efficient Green Parallel Computing for Educational Computing Terminals
Heterogeneous Distributed Computing Optimization for Diverse Educational IoT Devices
Distributed Metaverse Education Systems Supported by Cloud-Edge Architectures
Safety and Risk Assessment of Distributed Intelligent Education Service Systems
Paper Submission
All papers must be submitted electronically via EDAS at
https://edas.info/N35732
. Submissions must not substantially overlap with previously published papers or papers concurrently submitted to other journals or conferences. Special Session papers are limited to 6 pages, and must be formatted in the IEEE Computer Society Proceedings Format. Templates and formatting guidelines are available at
https://template-selector.ieee.org/
. All submitted papers will undergo a single-blind peer review process.
Paper Publication
Papers accepted by IEEE ISPA 2026 Special Session will be published by the IEEE Computer Society Press. All accepted papers will be submitted to IEEE Xplore and EI.
