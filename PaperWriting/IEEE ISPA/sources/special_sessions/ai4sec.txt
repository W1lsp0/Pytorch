AI4SEC 2026
AI4SEC 2026: IEEE ISPA 2026 Special Session on AI-Driven Optimization for Sustainable Edge-Cloud Continuum
The rapid proliferation of Internet of Things (IoT), 5G/6G networks, and data-intensive applications has accelerated the evolution of the edge-cloud continuum, where computation seamlessly spans from resource-constrained edge devices to powerful cloud infrastructures. While this paradigm enables low-latency services and scalable intelligence, it also introduces significant challenges in terms of energy consumption, resource allocation, system efficiency, and sustainability.
Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly machine learning and deep learning techniques, has emerged as a transformative enabler for optimizing the edge-cloud continuum. AI-driven approaches can dynamically manage resources, improve energy efficiency, enhance service quality, and support sustainable computing infrastructures.
This Special Session aims to bring together cutting-edge research and innovative solutions that leverage AI to optimize the performance, efficiency, and sustainability of edge-cloud systems. We invite original research articles, surveys, and application-driven studies addressing theoretical advancements, system architectures, and real-world deployments.
We invite submissions from academia, government and industry that present novel research on the topics as following areas:
1.AI for Resource Optimization
Intelligent resource provisioning in edge-cloud environments
Reinforcement learning for dynamic orchestration
Federated learning for distributed optimization
2.Energy Efficiency and Sustainability
Green AI for edge-cloud systems
Energy-aware computing and scheduling
Renewable energy integration in edge/cloud computing
3.Edge Intelligence and Distributed AI
Collaborative intelligence between edge and cloud
Model partitioning and split learning
On-device AI and lightweight models
Privacy-preserving AI in edge environments
4.Network Optimization and Communication Efficiency
AI-driven network slicing and traffic management
Adaptive communication protocols
Latency-aware optimization strategies
5G/6G-enabled edge-cloud systems
5.Security, Privacy, and Trust
Secure AI frameworks for edge-cloud continuum
Privacy-preserving data analytics
Blockchain-enabled edge-cloud systems
6.Applications and Use Cases
Smart cities and intelligent transportation
Industrial IoT and Industry 4.0
Autonomous systems and robotics
7.System Architectures and Frameworks
Serverless and microservices in edge-cloud systems
Digital twins for system optimization
Benchmarking and performance evaluation
Paper Submission
All papers must be submitted electronically via EDAS at
https://edas.info/N35739
. Submissions must not substantially overlap with previously published papers or papers concurrently submitted to other journals or conferences. Special Session papers are limited to 6 pages, and must be formatted in the IEEE Computer Society Proceedings Format. Templates and formatting guidelines are available at
https://template-selector.ieee.org/
. All submitted papers will undergo a single-blind peer review process.
Paper Publication
Papers accepted by IEEE ISPA 2026 Special Session will be published by the IEEE Computer Society Press. All accepted papers will be submitted to IEEE Xplore and EI.
