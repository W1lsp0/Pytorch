IEEE AI for Science Congress 2026

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

27-30 December 2026

Join us at IEEE AI for Science Congress 2026

As artificial intelligence (AI) continues to drive advancements in scientific research, the AI for Science Congress provides a distinguished platform for collaboration and innovation at the intersection of AI and various scientific fields. This conference convenes leading researchers, industry experts, and practitioners to discuss state-of-the-art AI technologies that propel scientific discovery, tackle global challenges, and optimize computational efficiency across disciplines.

The AI for Science Congress 2026 which includes the 24th IEEE International Symposium on Parallel and Distributed Processing with Applications (ISPA 2026); the 16th IEEE International Conference on Big Data and Cloud Computing (BDCloud 2026); the 19th IEEE International Conference on Social Computing and Networking (SocialCom 2026); the 16th IEEE International Conference on Sustainable Computing and Communications (SustainCom 2026); the 19th IEEE International Conference on Security, Privacy and Anonymity in Computation, Communication and Storage (SpaCCS 2026).
