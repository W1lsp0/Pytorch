## CFP 主题

### Track 1 — Systems and Architectures

Cloud computing and data center technology；migration of computations；multi-cloud、cloud federation、interoperability；energy management and green computing；wireless and mobile networks；systems and architectures for AI；distributed/parallel algorithms and applications；social networks、crowdsourcing、P2P；distributed file/storage systems 和 I/O systems。

### Track 2 — Technologies and Tools

Parallel/distributed computing paradigms；novel parallel programming paradigms；cloud service programming models；code generation and optimization；parallel compilers；middleware and tools；scheduling and resource management；reliability、fault tolerance、dependability；generative AI、agents、world models。

### Track 3 — Applications and Services

High-performance scientific/engineering computing；grid and cluster computing；pervasive/ubiquitous computing；databases、data mining、data management；big data and business analytics；scientific cloud systems/services；Internet computing and web services；software-defined networks and applications；LLMs powered by parallel/distributed computing。

### Track 4 — Security and Block-chain

Security in parallel AI systems；**federated learning**；blockchain applications/services；blockchain security/privacy；blockchain in cyber-physical systems；blockchain scalability；blockchain in edge/cloud computing；decentralized blockchain-enabled AI systems。

## 投稿、评审和出版

- 入口：EDAS <https://edas.info/N35627>，所有论文电子提交。
- 原创性：不得与已经发表的论文或同时投往其他期刊/会议的论文存在 substantial overlap。
- 评审：single-blind peer review。
- 格式：IEEE Computer Society Proceedings Format，模板入口 <https://template-selector.ieee.org/>。
- 篇幅：full regular paper 8 页免费、最多购买 2 页（总计 10 页）；同一 CFP 还给出 regular/special-session 6+2 页（总计 8 页）的表述，稿件类型和页数必须在 EDAS 中确认。
- 出版：IEEE Computer Society Press；CFP 声明录用论文提交 IEEE Xplore 和 EI。
- 后续机会：杰出论文在进一步修改后可能获邀投往专刊。CFP 列出的方向包括 IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems、Journal of Systems Architecture、Big Data Mining and Analytics、Computer Standards & Interfaces，具体邀请不等同于保证录用。
